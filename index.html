Does the car come with a warranty?

Yes. Every car includes a 12-month engine and transmission warranty. An extended warranty option is also available.

Can I get a test drive at home?

Absolutely. In Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad we bring the car to your doorstep — no pressure.

Which documents are needed for a bank lease?

CNIC, 6 months of bank statements or salary slips, NTN (filer status), a utility bill and 2 passport photos. Approval usually takes 48 hours.

How does the trade-in of my old car work?

We inspect your car and offer a fair market price, which is deducted from the price of your new car.

Who handles transfer and registration?

Our team handles the entire transfer, token tax and registration process. You only need your original CNIC.
